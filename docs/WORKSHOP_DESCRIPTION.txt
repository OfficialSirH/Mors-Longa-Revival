[h1]Mors Longa Revival[/h1]
[i]"A long and painful death" in latin[/i]

A co-op port of Overdrive Studios' [b]Mors Longa[/b] by SirH to Teardown 2.x multiplayer (up to 4 players).

[b]Original mod:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2906369984]Workshop[/url] | [url=https://discord.gg/j3ZW4fBdDT]Discord[/url] | [url=https://steamcommunity.com/groups/OverdriveStudiosTD]Overdrive Studios Steam group[/url]
[b]Source code:[/b] [url=https://github.com/OfficialSirH/Mors-Longa-Revival]GitHub[/url]

[h2]AI DISCLOSURE[/h2]
This project's whole porting process was done with Claude.

[h2]About this port[/h2]
Mors Longa Revival brings the original to Teardown 2.x and its co-op multiplayer:
[list]
[*][b]Co-op for up to 4 players.[/b] Each player has their own inventory, health, injuries, guns and money; the road is generated ahead of the player furthest along, and players left behind are brought along. Only the host starts a run (the host's difficulty, rules, perks and garage apply).
[*][b]Vehicles with seats.[/b] Every drivable vehicle has seats; friendly NPCs ride along too.
[*][b]Mounted guns without the separate AVF mod.[/b] Technicals, tanks, BMPs, the Katyusha, the Kornet / AGS-30 tripods and the other armed vehicles work on their own: a gunner place and AVF's shells, ported with credit.
[*][b]Flyable AS350 helicopter[/b] with seats and a mouse-aim flight model (the enemy helicopters keep their original AI).
[*]Many original bugs fixed along the way. The full list of changes against the original is in [url=https://github.com/OfficialSirH/Mors-Longa-Revival/blob/revival/docs/CHANGES.md]CHANGES.md[/url], unfinished or set-aside content in [url=https://github.com/OfficialSirH/Mors-Longa-Revival/blob/revival/docs/STATUS.md]STATUS.md[/url].
[/list]

[b]Status:[/b] Everything is tested in single-player. Multiplayer has been tested to a limited extent with two players within the local multiplayer. There's a rare crash where the game closes without an error, cause not found yet.
Not yet in the port: the CH-47 and Hind helicopters (single-player only for now), horde mode (unfinished in the original too), the unfinished PKM, M9, Bauer-553 and MGL guns, and the AGS-30 aiming up and down.

[h2]Story[/h2]
The Country of Alashya, heavily influenced by America in the middle east, has entered lockdown due to a deadly, paranoia inducing virus. Civilians are outraged, attempting to flee the country, but the Alashyan government has shut down the inner cities and rural areas making it almost impossible to leave. Insurgents and anarchists have risen up. The population is frantic as everyone is trying escape Alashya. The AAF forces have now been given the green light to eliminate anyone attempting to escape through the country's vast highways with lethal force.

[h2]Gameplay objective[/h2]
Now since the highway is endless and you literally cannot finish the game, your task will be to survive as long as you can, by continuing to move forwards, finding food and fuel to keep you going. Search abandoned roadside buildings for loot, optimize your inventory storage and tread carefully, as getting shot can lead to bleeding damage and bone fractures. And lastly, kill anyone that means you harm.

[h2]Temporary bug solutions[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Inventory flush[/b] – drop everything in your inventory by holding D+R+O and pressing P last, with the other keys still held down (affects only your own inventory).
[*][b]Continue button greyed out[/b] when it shouldn't be – hold C+T+N in the main menu (the host; only the host starts the game).
[*][b]NPCs lying on the ground inactive[/b] – press Backspace (debug menu) → Reset NPCs.
[/list]
